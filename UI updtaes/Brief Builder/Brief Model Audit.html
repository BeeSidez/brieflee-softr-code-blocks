Headline: Hook, Talking Points, Script, CTAs and Guidelines are already built — they're just hidden inside the Brief section's JSON cards rather than surfaced as toggleable sections. The real content gaps are The Offer, Creator Benefits, Props, Usage Rights, Contact/Community, plus a proper Audience block.

CNew since the Bible fold in

Concepts the live app added that the Bible doesn't mention — keep these in the merged model.

  • Review Mode — Autonomous / Hybrid / Manual (who has the final say on a submission).
  • Review Agents (26) + 9 thresholds — the QA engine. This is what powers the creator-side "What our AI checks / Bars to hit".
  • Awareness stage (5) + Angle (16) — the marketing framing that drives hook/format suggestions.
  • Filmer (Influencer / UGC / Founder / Customer / Employee) and Channel (Organic / Paid).
  • Status, Privacy/SEO, Thumbnail, Brief PDF, and the Storyboard's 18 section types.

DRedundant / renamed / to reconcile

  • Three notions of "format". "Content style" (8: Product demo, Testimonial, Unboxing…) vs the create-flow format field vs the Bible's 42-item Visual Format library. Pick one canonical field; the 8 content-styles look like a subset of the 42.
  • "QA Checklist" → "Review Agents". UI renamed; DB field qa_checklist unchanged. Old label is dead.
  • "Filmer" vs "who leads". The Bible frames Talking Points vs Script by creator-led vs brand-led; the app models the person as creator_type/Filmer. Decide if these are the same axis.
  • Audience overlap. "Awareness stage" (marketing funnel) and a future Audience section both describe the viewer — keep them distinct or merge.
  • Font mismatch. The live editor renders in League Spartan; this design system is Inter. Align on one.
  • Legacy folders present: archive/thresholds-legacy, archive/paywall, archive/upgrade — dead, ignore.

EDirection for the merged builder

What I'll build once you confirm — combining today's editor with the section/module concept.

  • One surface = the white "page" editor with per-section collapse/expand toggles (already the live pattern) + the Sections/Modules rail to add or remove any section. Fully customizable — Creator Info, Contact, etc. are optional; add only what this brief needs.
  • Canonical section set = today's 6 structural sections + the Bible's missing content sections (Offer, Benefits, Props, Usage Rights, Audience, Contact/Community), all as toggleable blocks.
  • Preview button → the creator-side view (hero + brand, submission details, "What our AI checks" driven by the thresholds, inspiration, Submit). Different design, page-like, read-only.
  • Modules stay the pre-generated workspace source (brand blueprint / product / concepts / hooks / formats) that fills sections; Sections start empty with AI assist.
