# Score Your Video: copy review and rewrite (manager door)

Reviewed 2026-07-31 against the 7 scoring agents in `Brieflee/ad-system/scoring-agents.md`.

**Audience reviewed for:** the people who manage creators. TikTok Shop affiliate managers, TSPs and agencies running affiliate programmes, UGC and influencer managers, in-house marketers running creator content. Not creators.

---

## The headline finding

The copy under review is the **creator door** (its own header says "Creator door" and "the creator variant"). It is internally consistent and well written for that reader. It is the wrong reader.

Evidence that it is creator-shaped, not manager-shaped:

| Line | Who it speaks to |
|---|---|
| "Score **your** video." | The person who made it |
| "show you exactly what works and **what to fix**" | The person who will re-edit it |
| "Download it as an image **for stories or feed**" | Someone posting to their own audience |
| "what to **re-shoot or re-caption**" | The person holding the camera |
| "your own MP4 **straight off your phone**" | Filming on mobile |
| "Copyright flags **before you post**" | The publisher |
| "Who uses it" leads with **Creators** | Priority order |
| "**Work with brands?** Review every creator video in one place" | A cross-link written *to* a creator |

Meanwhile the design brief specifies the opposite page: *"A free, public tool for brands and agencies... Who lands here: a brand marketer or agency running creator content"*, with the headline *"Score your creator's video. Free."*

**Consequence beyond tone:** the gate rejects free email domains. On a creator door that blocks most creators after they have already waited for a score. On a manager door it is correct and even useful as a qualifier. Switching audience resolves this.

## The two strategic gaps

Both matter more than the line edits.

**1. The manager's actual pain is missing.** Everything the research says about this buyer is volume, time and consistency: watching forty videos to find the six with a problem, and the bar moving depending on who reviewed it that day. The page sells depth (24 agents, timestamps, granularity) which is impressive but is not what they lie awake about. The single best manager line on the whole page is buried in section 5: *"Spot-check a creator delivery in a minute instead of watching it three times."* That is the promise, and it is in the wrong place.

**2. The brief is never mentioned, so the upgrade argument is weak.** Brieflee's differentiator is checking against **your brief**. This tool checks against general best practice, which is correct for a free tool. That gap is the reason to upgrade, and stating it plainly makes the free tool honest and the product desirable at the same time. Currently the FAQ bridges on volume ("across every video your creators send you"), which is the weaker half of the argument.

## 2. What you get

**Section tag:** What you get
**Heading:** A read you can act on, in less time than watching it once.
**Sub:** One score out of 100, then a check-by-check breakdown from all 24 Review Agents, with a timestamp on every moment that needs a second look.

### Card 1
**Visual:** score ring at 82, "Video score / 19 of 24 checks passed"
**Title:** A score card you can send
**Body:** One number out of 100 and the headline checks, built to drop straight into Slack, a client report, or a message back to the creator. Download it as an image.

### Card 2
**Visual:** three check rows: Hook quality (Pass), Brand mention (Flag), Safe zones (Flag)
**Title:** All 24 checks, grouped
**Body:** Hook and attention, creator and delivery, production quality, brand and conversion. Each agent gives a pass or a flag and says why in one line.

### Card 3
**Visual:** video frame beside a flag note: "0:18 Brand mention / First mention lands past the point most viewers leave."
**Title:** Timestamps, not opinions
**Body:** Every flag points at the second it happens, with the frame beside it, so your feedback names a moment instead of a feeling.

### Benefits grid (six)
1. **Hook checks on the first seconds** — Visual hook, opening line and pacing, scored against what holds attention.
2. **Watchable on mute** — Captions, on-screen text and visual storytelling reviewed for sound-off viewers.
3. **Audio and delivery** — Voice clarity, music balance, pronunciation and energy, all flagged with timestamps.
4. **Safe zones and legibility** — Where captions and buttons cover the text, and where it drops out of the frame.
5. **Brand and conversion checks** — Product visibility, brand mention timing, CTA strength and brand alignment.
6. **Copyright flags before it goes live** — Sound and footage checked so nothing gets pulled after it posts.

## 6. FAQ

**Section tag:** Questions
**Heading:** Before you paste the link.

**Is it really free?**
Yes. Scoring a video costs nothing and you do not need an account to see the score. You give a work email only if you want the full check-by-check breakdown from all 24 Review Agents.

**Which videos can I score?**
Any short-form video: TikTok, Instagram Reel, YouTube Short or Facebook Reel. Paste the link, or upload the file a creator sent you.

**How long does it take?**
About a minute. The 24 agents watch in parallel, so a 30 second clip and a 3 minute clip finish at roughly the same time.

**What is a Review Agent?**
One agent, one job. Instead of a single vague verdict, each agent checks one thing, such as whether the brand name is said early enough, or whether the captions sit inside the safe zone. That is why every flag comes with a timestamp.

**Why a work email?**
Free email domains are not accepted. The breakdown is built for people reviewing other people's content, so the gate keeps it to working inboxes.

**What happens to the video?**
[CONFIRM ACTUAL BEHAVIOUR BEFORE PUBLISHING. Managers upload client content that has not been released, so this needs a real answer: how long it is kept, who can see it, whether it trains anything. A vague retention-policy line will cost more conversions than a plain honest one.]

**What is the difference between this and Brieflee?**
This scores one video against general best practice. Brieflee scores every video against **your brief**, so the checks are your requirements rather than generic ones, and it tracks every creator and every submission in one place. Free for 7 days.

## What still needs you

1. **The retention answer.** Flagged above. It is the one place the page currently says nothing, and it is a genuine objection for someone uploading a client's unreleased content.
2. **Keep or kill the creator door.** The copy you sent is good creator copy. If both doors run, the creator variant needs its own email gate that accepts personal addresses, or it will block the people it is written for.
3. **The lead magnet contradicts one line in the brand bible**, which bans "upload your own video to score it" framing on the grounds that the brand is the reader and creators are a separate party. The manager door resolves this: the manager uploads their creator's video, not their own. Worth noting if a creator door ever ships.
