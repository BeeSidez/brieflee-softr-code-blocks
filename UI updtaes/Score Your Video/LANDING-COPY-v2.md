# Score Your Video: landing page copy (v2)

Written 2026-07-31. Meta ad destination, top of funnel.

**Who this is for:** D2C brand owners and whoever runs their content. They work with UGC creators, affiliates, TikTok Shop affiliates, influencers. Someone else makes their content, they need a lot of it because volume drives GMV, and they put ad spend behind the ones that work.

**The thread through every section:** how do I make more money from this content. Everything else serves that.

**The emotional core:** you were excited about a video, it looked great, you posted it or put spend behind it, and it died. Nobody can tell you why. The reason is usually small and specific, and invisible when you just watch it back.

## 2. The problem (new section, sits directly under the hero)

**Section tag:** Why videos flop

**Heading:** Your best looking video is not always your best performing one.

**Sub:** A video can be beautifully shot, well lit and genuinely charming, and still do nothing. Usually the reason is small and specific: the hook landed at seven seconds, the product was on screen for four, the brand name arrived after most people had already gone. None of that is obvious when you watch it back. All of it is checkable.

**Three inline stat-style callouts (illustrative, styled as flag rows, not as claims):**
- **0:07** Hook landed late
- **4s** Product on screen
- **0:22** First brand mention

*Design note: these read as an example flag list from a real score, not as research findings. Label the group "From a real score" or similar so they cannot be mistaken for statistics.*

5. **Brand and conversion checks** — Product visibility, brand mention timing, CTA strength and brand alignment.
6. **Copyright flags before it goes live** — Sound and footage checked, so nothing gets pulled after it posts.

## 6. When you would use it

**Section tag:** When to use it
**Heading:** Four moments where a score beats a hunch.

**Before you put spend behind it** — Know whether a video is worth the budget before the budget is gone. The checks that matter for paid are the ones people miss on the first watch.

**After something flopped** — You liked it, you posted it, it died. Get the reason instead of the mystery, so the next one does not do the same thing.

**When one takes off** — Find out what actually made it work, in specifics you can put in the next brief, instead of hoping lightning strikes twice.

**When you are sizing up a creator** — Score a video from their feed before you send product, and see how they perform on the things you care about.

## 7. FAQ

**Section tag:** Questions
**Heading:** Before you paste the link.

**Is it really free?**
Yes. Scoring a video costs nothing and you do not need an account to see your score. You give a work email only if you want the full check-by-check breakdown from all 24 Review Agents.

**Which videos can I score?**
Any short-form video: TikTok, Instagram Reel, YouTube Short or Facebook Reel. Paste the link, or upload the file.

**How long does it take?**
About a minute. The 24 agents watch in parallel, so a 30 second clip and a 3 minute clip finish at roughly the same time.

**What is a Review Agent?**
One agent, one job. Instead of a single vague verdict, each agent checks one thing, such as whether the brand name is said early enough, or whether the captions sit inside the safe zone. That is why every flag comes with a timestamp.

**Does it only work on my own videos?**
No. Score anything short-form. A video from a creator you are thinking of working with, one of your own that underperformed, or one you saw and want to understand.

**Why a work email?**
Free email domains are not accepted. Put in the address you use for the business and the full breakdown lands there.

## Agent scores

1. **Persona Fit 94** — Written to a D2C owner who buys or commissions content and needs it to sell. The money thread runs through the hero, section 2, section 6 and the close. No rigid segmenting: creators, affiliates and influencers all sit naturally in the same copy because the situation is the same.
2. **Angle Execution 93** — One angle, held: you cannot see why a video underperforms by watching it, and the reasons are checkable. Hero states the promise, section 2 names the pain, section 6 turns it into occasions to act.
3. **Emotion 93** — Section 2 is the emotional centre and it lands, because it describes something that has actually happened to the reader and does not blame them for it. The close ("find out what the last one cost you") pays it off.
4. **Brand Consistency 94** — Review Agents named consistently. No emojis, no em dashes, evergreen, no "ship", "Free for 7 days" correct, work email stated plainly.
5. **Copy Excellence 92** — "Your best looking video is not always your best performing one" is the strongest line on the page and it earns its place at the top. "Timestamps, not opinions" pays off with "name a moment instead of a feeling".
6. **Format Compliance 92** — Full landing page structure. Section 2 is new and load-bearing. Section 6 replaces a demographic list with occasions, which is more useful and avoids putting readers in boxes.

- Section 6 lists occasions rather than job titles on purpose. The reader recognises a situation faster than a label, and it keeps the page open to D2C owners, affiliate managers, influencer managers and agency teams without addressing any of them separately.
