## 5. What Facebook ads check that organic does not

**This section is the point of this page. Nothing here appears on the TikTok, Reels, Shorts or breakdown pages.**

**Section tag:** Built for ads
**Heading:** One video. Placements you did not choose.

**Sub:** A creator makes one video. Meta then decides where it runs, and crops it to fit. A composition that works full screen in your editor can lose its text, its product or its logo in a placement you never looked at. These are the checks that only matter once money is behind it.

**One video, many shapes** — Feed, Reels, Stories and in-stream all crop differently. The agents check whether the important parts survive every crop, not just the one you designed for.

**Automatic placement means automatic cropping** — When Meta distributes the creative across placements, you do not get to approve each version. The check happens before, or it does not happen.

**Sound off by default** — Most of the feed watches on mute. Captions, on-screen text and whether the video makes sense without audio are worth more here than on platforms where people arrive with sound on.

**The first three seconds cost money here** — On organic a slow hook loses reach. On paid it loses budget, because you pay for the impression whether or not anyone stays.

**Before ad review, not after** — Claims and copyright get flagged while the video is still yours to change, rather than after a rejection.

**Partnership Ads run from the creator's handle** — The video represents them and you at the same time. Worth knowing what is in it before it carries two names.

## 8. FAQ

**Section tag:** Questions
**Heading:** Things people ask before they paste.

**Is the Facebook ad video checker really free?**
Yes. Paste a URL, give a work email, and all 24 checks come back with timestamps. No card, no account.

**Can I check a video before it runs as an ad?**
That is what it is for. Upload the file a creator sent you and check it before any budget goes near it. Nothing needs to be live first.

**Does it check the safe zones for different placements?**
Yes, and it is the check people find most useful here. Feed, Reels and Stories crop differently, so text that sits fine full screen can fall outside the frame in a placement you did not design for.

**Will it tell me if my ad is going to be rejected?**
It flags risky claims and copyright issues in the words and the sound, which is where most avoidable problems come from. It is not a substitute for Meta's ad review, it is a pass before it.

**Can I check a competitor's ad?**
Yes. Any public Facebook video. Paste the URL.

**How long does it take?**
Under 60 seconds for most videos. The 24 agents watch in parallel.

**Can ChatGPT do this from a link?**
No. General assistants can discuss a video you describe to them. This watches the file, times the hook and reports what it found with timestamps.

**Why do you ask for a work email?**
Free email addresses are not accepted. The full check is built for people running brand content, so the gate keeps it to working inboxes.

**What is the difference between this and Brieflee?**
This is one video. Brieflee runs the same 24 Review Agents across every video your creators send you, checked against your brief rather than general best practice, so nothing goes out or goes behind spend without being checked first.
