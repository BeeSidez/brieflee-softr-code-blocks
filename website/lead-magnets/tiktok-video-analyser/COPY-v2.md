## 7. FAQ

**Section tag:** Questions
**Heading:** Things people ask before they paste.

**Is the TikTok video analyser really free?**
Yes. Paste a link and the score appears without an account. You give a work email only if you want the full check-by-check breakdown from all 24 Review Agents.

**Can I analyse someone else's TikTok?**
Yes. Any public TikTok. Most people run a mix: videos of their own that underperformed, and videos from other accounts they want to learn from.

**How long does it take?**
Under 60 seconds for most clips. The 24 agents watch in parallel, so a 15 second video and a 3 minute one finish at roughly the same time.

**What does the AI actually detect?**
Computer vision reads every frame for scene changes, on-screen text, product visibility, lighting and framing. Speech to text reads the audio for the brand mention, the CTA and delivery. Each of the 24 Review Agents checks one of those and reports a pass or a flag with the timestamp.

**Can ChatGPT analyse a TikTok like this?**
Not from a link, and not frame by frame. General assistants can discuss a video you describe to them. This watches the file, times the hook and reports what it found with timestamps.

**Is there a limit on video length?**
Short-form clips up to three minutes.

**Why do you ask for a work email?**
Free email addresses are not accepted. The full breakdown is built for people working on brand content, so the gate keeps it to working inboxes.
