# Video Breakdown: page copy v3

For `/free-tool-video-breakdown`. Written 2026-07-31. Sister page to the TikTok analyser v3, same avatar, platform-agnostic.

## The avatar

**Someone who manages creators for brands and has a lot of content to watch.**

A creator sends a video. They open it, watch it at speed, half-looking for whether the product is on screen enough, whether the brand got said, whether the hook is there, whether anything in it could cause a problem. Then they approve it, or write feedback. Then they do it again. Some of these are about to have money put behind them.

Test applied to every line: **would someone who has never had to review another person's video write this?** If yes, it was cut.

**Where this page stands:** 551 clicks from 10,189 impressions, 5.4% CTR. More impressions than any page on the site, roughly half the click rate of the TikTok analyser. Some of that gap is unavoidable, since "video breakdown" is a broad query pulling people who want film analysis and editing tutorials. Fix the page first, test the listing after.

**SEO retained:** "break down", "frame by frame", the 60 seconds promise, the comparison table, the frame-by-frame deep dive.

## 10. FAQ

**Section tag:** Questions
**Heading:** Things people ask before they paste.

**Is the video breakdown tool really free?**
Yes. Paste a URL and the score appears without an account. You give a work email only if you want the full check-by-check breakdown from all 24 Review Agents.

**Can I run a video a creator sent me privately?**
Yes. Upload the file instead of pasting a link. Nothing needs to be posted first.

**Which videos can I break down?**
Any short-form video: TikTok, Instagram Reel, YouTube Short or Facebook Reel. Paste the URL, or upload the file.

**Can I break down someone else's video?**
Yes. Any public short-form video. Most people run a mix: videos their own creators made, and videos from accounts they are thinking of working with.

**How long does it take?**
Under 60 seconds for most clips. The 24 agents watch in parallel, so a 15 second video and a 3 minute one finish at roughly the same time.

**What does the AI actually detect?**
Computer vision reads every frame for scene changes, on-screen text, product visibility, lighting and framing. Speech to text reads the audio for the brand mention, the CTA and delivery. Each of the 24 Review Agents checks one of those and reports a pass or a flag with the timestamp.

**Can ChatGPT do this from a link?**
No. General assistants can discuss a video you describe to them. This watches the file, times the hook, measures how long the product is on screen and reports what it found with timestamps.

**Can I use the flags as feedback for the creator?**
That is what most people do with them. Each flag is a timestamp and a one-line reason, so it goes straight into a message without you rewriting it.

**Is there a limit on video length?**
Short-form clips up to three minutes.

**Why do you ask for a work email?**
Free email addresses are not accepted. The full breakdown is built for people reviewing other people's content, so the gate keeps it to working inboxes.

**What is the difference between this and Brieflee?**
This is one video. Brieflee runs the same 24 Review Agents across every video your creators send you, checked against your brief rather than general best practice, so the pile arrives already sorted and you only watch what needs you. Free for 7 days.
