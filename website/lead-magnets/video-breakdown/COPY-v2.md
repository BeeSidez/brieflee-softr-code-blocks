For `/free-tool-video-breakdown`. Written 2026-07-31. Sister page to the TikTok analyser copy, same structure, platform-agnostic.

**Where this page stands:** 551 clicks from 10,189 impressions, 5.4% CTR. More impressions than any other page on the site and roughly half the click rate of the TikTok analyser.

---

## First, an honest correction on the CTR gap

I said earlier that closing the gap to the TikTok page's 9.9% would take this page to around 1,000 clicks. That was too confident, and worth correcting before you act on it.

"Video breakdown" is a far broader query than "tiktok video analyzer". People typing it want film analysis, sports breakdowns, editing tutorials, cost breakdowns. A chunk of those 10,189 impressions are people who were never going to click, and no title fixes that. High impressions with a lower click rate is partly the natural shape of ranking for a broad head term.

So the title is still worth sharpening, but treat it as a few points of CTR rather than a doubling. The bigger prize on this page is what happens *after* the click, which is the same problem as the TikTok page: the search listing promises a free breakdown in 60 seconds, and the page currently delivers a loading animation, an email gate, a signup and a card.

**Recommended title (55):** `Free Video Breakdown Tool | AI Frame-by-Frame | Brieflee`

This mirrors the exact pattern of the page that converts at 9.9%. "AI Hook + CTA Score" is insider language for a broad-intent query; "AI Frame-by-Frame" is concrete, and it is also a term you separately rank for.

**Recommended description (147):** `Paste any TikTok, Instagram Reel or YouTube Short and 24 AI agents watch it, scoring the hook, structure, audio and CTA in 60 seconds. Free to use.`

The change that matters is "24 AI agents watch it". Nothing else in those search results says a number of agents will watch the video for you, and it puts the house verb into the snippet.

**Test one at a time.** Title first, leave it four weeks, then the description. Changing both means you learn nothing about which moved it.

## 9. FAQ

**Section tag:** Questions
**Heading:** Things people ask before they paste.

**Is the video breakdown tool really free?**
Yes. Paste a URL and the score appears without an account. You give a work email only if you want the full check-by-check breakdown from all 24 Review Agents.

**Which videos can I break down?**
Any short-form video: TikTok, Instagram Reel, YouTube Short or Facebook Reel. Paste the URL, or upload the file.

**Can I break down someone else's video?**
Yes. Any public short-form video. Most people run a mix: videos of their own that underperformed, and videos from other accounts they want to learn from.

**How long does it take?**
Under 60 seconds for most clips. The 24 agents watch in parallel, so a 15 second video and a 3 minute one finish at roughly the same time.

**What does the AI actually detect?**
Computer vision reads every frame for scene changes, on-screen text, product visibility, lighting and framing. Speech to text reads the audio for the brand mention, the CTA and delivery. Each of the 24 Review Agents checks one of those and reports a pass or a flag with the timestamp.

**Can ChatGPT break down a video like this?**
Not from a URL, and not frame by frame. General assistants can discuss a video you describe to them. This watches the file, times the hook and reports what it found with timestamps.

**Is there a limit on video length?**
Short-form clips up to three minutes.

**Why do you ask for a work email?**
Free email addresses are not accepted. The full breakdown is built for people working on brand content, so the gate keeps it to working inboxes.

**What is the difference between this and Brieflee?**
This breaks down one video. Brieflee runs the same 24 Review Agents across every video your creators send you, so nothing goes out or goes behind spend without being checked first. Free for 7 days.
