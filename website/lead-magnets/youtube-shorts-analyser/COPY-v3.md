## 5. What Shorts check that other platforms do not

**This section is the point of this page. Nothing here appears on the TikTok, Reels or breakdown pages.**

**Section tag:** Built for Shorts
**Heading:** Shorts loop, and YouTube is a search engine.

**Sub:** A video that works as a Reel can underperform as a Short, because Shorts replay on their own, carry a visible title, and get found by people searching rather than scrolling. These are the checks that only matter here.

**The loop** — Shorts replay automatically, so the last frame runs straight into the first. When the ending carries back round, people watch twice without deciding to. When it cuts hard, the loop gives them the exact moment to leave. The agents check whether the ending sets up the replay or ends it.

**The title over the frame** — Your title sits over the video and is doing search work as well as sitting in the composition. Text placed low ends up competing with it.

**The Shorts interface** — The subscribe button, the channel handle and the action rail all take up frame. The safe zones are not the same as TikTok's or Instagram's.

**The opening frame as a thumbnail** — Shorts surface in the shelf and in YouTube search, where the first frame works as a still. A frame that reads as a hook in motion does not always read as anything at rest.

**Length against retention** — Shorts run up to three minutes, and longer is not free. The agents check whether the length is earned or whether attention drops well before the end.

**Spoken clarity for search** — YouTube reads what is said. Clear delivery does more work here than on platforms where audio is decoration.
