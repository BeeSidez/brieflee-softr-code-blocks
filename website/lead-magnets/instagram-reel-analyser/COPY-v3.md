## 5. What Reels check that other platforms do not

**This section is the point of this page. Nothing here appears on the TikTok, Shorts or breakdown pages.**

**Section tag:** Built for Reels
**Heading:** Instagram covers, crops and rewards differently.

**Sub:** A video that works on TikTok can quietly fall apart as a Reel, because Instagram puts its interface in different places and shows the same video in more than one shape. These are the checks that only matter here.

**The caption and audio strip** — Instagram's caption block and audio attribution run along the bottom and cover more of the frame than people expect. Text placed low reads fine in the editor and disappears in the feed.

**The right-hand action rail** — Like, comment, share and the menu run up the right side. Anything important on that edge competes with buttons.

**The grid crop** — Reels get cropped for the profile grid. A composition centred for 9:16 can lose its subject in the grid tile, which is where a lot of people meet your content first.

**The cover frame** — The opening frame does double duty as the cover image. A frame that works as a hook does not always work as a thumbnail.

**Original audio versus trending audio** — Whether the audio choice suits the Reel, and whether the voiceover survives being played against a trending track.

**Saves and shares** — Instagram rewards content people save and send on. The agents flag the moments built for that, and the ones that read as an ad and get scrolled.
